Mandatory dos + don'ts

Do

  • Show the jar clearly, branding facing the correct direction
  • Use the full product name: RevitaLift Triple Power Anti-Aging Moisturizer
  • Product looks its best: clean jar, no smudges or dents
  • Zoom in on the texture and on your skin during application
  • Share your honest, truthful experience with the product
  • Make only claims included in this brief or on pack, with their qualifiers, and only if they reflect your experience
  • Use music only from the commercial music library
  • Include your FTC verbal disclosure and text overlay. If you're paid beyond commission and free product, say "I'm partnering with", not "gifted"
  • Pricing: use the TikTok Shop price as confirmed by TikTok Shop, or our MSRP clearly labeled "MSRP"
  • Keep text overlays within media-safe zones

Don't

  • No medical or drug claims; don't say it "treats", "cures" or replaces procedures
  • No before-and-after claims unless results are real and unedited
  • No third-party marks, logos or other people
  • No children or animals
  • No filters, AI, color effects or retouching
  • No filming in-store
  • No distracting backgrounds; keep it clean and simple
  • No political or religious affiliations
  • No swearing or offensive language
  • No alcohol or drug paraphernalia
