Content guidelines

Content musts

  • Must ensure you’re saying/captioning the full product name and shade
  • Integrate the product naturally within first 3-5 seconds.
  • In your own words, highlight your favorite product benefits.
  • Zoom in on face to capture close-up of product application.

Mandatory disclosures

  • Include the right overlay and caption to be eligible for media sparking
  • In Video: NYX Cosmetics Partner must be added as a text overlay for the duration of the video
  • Caption: #NYXCosmeticsPartner, @nyxcosmetics
  • Any reference to the brand must be NYX Professional Makeup – not ‘NYX’
