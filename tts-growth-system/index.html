Step 3 · AI winning creative

Proven winners, and the ones nobody has pushed yet.

The AI reads two things for every video: what it has already sold on TikTok Shop, and how likely it is to sell on another channel. Select a video to see its scorecard.

Step 4 · Request usage rights

A deal creators say yes to.

We ask for usage rights on each selected video. In return, the creator gets a flat fee and a share of the ad spend that runs behind their content.

Rights

Permission to run the video as a paid ad, and on landing pages, for an agreed term.

Flat fee

A fixed payment per approved video, paid whatever the ads go on to do.

% of spend

A percentage of the ad spend behind the video, so creators earn more when their content scales.

Creator earns $1,500

$500 flat fee plus $1,000 from 5% of $20,000 in spend. Example terms; set per brand.

Step 5 · Build pages

A page for every product. Or every video.

Each landing page is generated from the winning video: the creator's clip, the hook they used and the product they showed. The ad and the page say the same thing, so nothing changes between the click and the checkout.

Step 5 · Run ads

Meta to DTC. TikTok to Amazon. And so on.

Every selected video runs where the data says it sells. Line widths show where spend settled in the flow above. Hover or tap a route.

Step 6 · Measure ROAS lift

Lift, measured against your own baseline.

We compare ROAS on every page and ad against what the brand gets today, across the board, and move budget on what we find.

Baseline

The brand's current ROAS on each channel before launch.

Unit

Every landing page, video and ad set is measured on its own.

Read-out

Lift versus baseline by product, video, page and channel.

Decision

Scale what lifts, pause what doesn't, and feed results into the next selection.
