If the top GMV creator drives $25,000 or more, they will be eligible to win the trip to Paris, and the designer luxury bag will be awarded to the next top performer. If the top GMV creator drives under $25,000, they will be eligible to win the designer luxury bag, and the trip to Paris will not be awarded.
