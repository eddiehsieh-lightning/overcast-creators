All about the products

Both items come in the bundle. Talk about them in your own words. Use these benefits as a guide, not a script.

Step 1 · Matte blurring liner

Blurfiller Blurring Lip Contour

Fuller-looking lips. The creamy formula and rounded tip make application effortless.

  • Effortless applicationRounded dome tip for seamless application, no blending needed
  • Rich creamy formulaGlides along the lips without tugging or dragging
  • Soft textureSmooths lips for a soft blurred look with a natural finish
Application tip

Pick a shade slightly deeper than your natural lip color. Apply just above the cupid's bow and beneath the center of the lower lip. Mimic the appearance of shadow for an instantly fuller lip look.

Step 2 · Color-shifting lip oil · 0.16 fl oz

Plump Ambition pH Hyaluron Lip Oil

Color-shifting, glass-like shine with a lasting tint of unique pink. 24HR hydration and plumper-looking lips.

  • 24HR hydrationFor nourished, comfortable lips
  • 2% hyaluronic acid + tripeptidesTo smooth and plump
  • Volumizing, glass-like shineLightweight, non-sticky formula
  • Color-shifting formulaAdapts to your pH for a custom pink tint
  • Cushiony pillow applicatorMassaging beads for a luxurious feel
  • Available in 4 shades
How to apply
  1. Using the cushiony pillow applicator, apply starting in the center of your upper lip.
  2. Work from the center to the outer edges of your lips, then glide across the entire bottom lip.
  3. Wait at least 10 minutes to assess your unique color and glassy, plumper-looking lips.

Dos + don'ts

Do
  • Show both steps: Blurfiller first, then Plump Ambition
  • Use the full official product names: Blurfiller Blurring Lip Contour and Plump Ambition pH Hyaluron Lip Oil
  • Show the products being applied to your lips, close up
  • Display the products clearly: logo legible, packaging in good condition
  • Film vertically (9:16) in high resolution, well lit and in focus
  • Share your honest, truthful experience with the products. Let your personality shine through
  • Make only the performance claims included in this brief or on pack, and only if they reflect your actual experience
  • Include any necessary product-use instructions and warnings
  • Use music only from the commercial music library
  • Include your FTC verbal disclosure and text overlay, using the full brand name: L'Oréal Paris
  • If you're paid beyond commission and free product, say "I'm partnering with" in your verbal disclosure. Don't describe it as gifted
  • If you mention price, use the TikTok Shop price as confirmed by TikTok Shop, or the MSRP clearly labeled "MSRP"
  • Clearly state any limitations on promoted offers
  • Keep all text overlays within media-safe zones so captions won't block them
Don't
  • No third-party marks, logos or other people
  • No exaggerated or unrealistic claims
  • No filters, AI, color effects or retouching
  • Don't film in-store
  • No distracting backgrounds or styling; keep it clean and simple
  • No inappropriate imagery (overly revealing clothing, excessive skin, PDA)
  • No children or animals
  • No political or religious affiliations
  • No swearing or offensive language
  • No alcohol or drug paraphernalia
