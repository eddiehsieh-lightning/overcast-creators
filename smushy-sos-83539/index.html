Filming do's & don'ts

✓Filming do's

Mandatory disclosure

Include the correct overlay and caption to be eligible for media sparking:

  • In Video: NYX Cosmetics Partner must be added as a text overlay for the duration of the video
  • Caption: #NYXCosmeticsPartner @nyxcosmetics

Any reference to the brand must be NYX Professional Makeup – not ‘NYX’. ‘NYX’ is pronounced “Nicks”.

Content do's

  • Only include honest and truthful experiences with the products
  • Use the inverted filter so product name is readable on screen
  • Specify what shade you are wearing.
  • Make sure all content is in focus (not blurry), and all products are represented in a positive way — no smudges, rips, or dents in packaging, etc. All products should be clearly in focus, well lit, NOT upside down
  • Remove all stickers / barcodes from product
  • Include an original VO recording, or licensed music provided by the brand
  • Shoot content from a straight on, front facing angle in a well lit area
  • Show the product in front of the camera before application
  • Please make sure all content is high-res

✕Filming don'ts

  • Do not refer to brand as “NYX,” only “NYX Professional Makeup”
  • No comparison to a medical procedure (lip filler, face lift etc)
  • No distracting backgrounds, keep clean and simple
  • No branded clothing or clothing with visible logos
  • DO NOT call out, mention or feature any products that are not NYX Professional Makeup. You will need to use NYX PMU for ALL products called out, mentioned, or featured within the video.
  • No Filters, color effects or excessive retouching
  • Content should not be shot from a sideways or tilted angle
  • No personal logos, watermarks, gifs, full body shots or prominent text
